## 1. Syllabus Analytics Now Displays Real Data
* **The Problem:** When you opened the "Syllabus Analytics" tab, the graphs were blank and said *"No lessons registered"*, making it impossible to see student progress.
* **The Solution:** We resolved a connection issue behind the scenes. The charts will now properly display how many students started and finished each lesson, giving you clear insights into student engagement.
* **The Benefit for You:** Instantly see which lessons students love and where students might be getting stuck, with zero blank screens.

---

## 2. Permanent Lesson & Chapter Assignment
* **The Problem:** Sometimes after assigning lessons to a chapter/module, they would unexpectedly unassign or reset, forcing you to link them over and over.
* **The Solution:** We discovered that saving a lesson was accidentally clearing its chapter tag. We have locked this relationship in place so once a lesson is in a chapter, it stays there permanently until you deliberately move it.
* **The Benefit for You:** No more lost work or having to re-assign lessons repeatedly.

---

## 3. Quick Rename on the Course Page (No Popups)
* **The Problem:** To fix a simple typo or rename a lesson, you previously had to click "Edit", wait for a large form window to open, scroll, change the title, and click "Save".
* **The Solution:** You can now rename any lesson right on the course page. Simply click the title or the small pencil icon, type the new name, and press **Enter**.
* **The Benefit for You:** Updating 10 lesson titles will take 30 seconds instead of 5 minutes.

---

## 4. Automatic Video Duration Sync
* **The Problem:** Every time you add a lesson, you had to manually calculate or guess the video length in minutes.
* **The Solution:** When you paste your video link or ID (from Bunny Stream, YouTube, or direct upload), the system automatically detects the exact video length and fills in the duration for you.
* **The Benefit for You:** 100% accurate lesson times across your curriculum with zero manual math.

---

## 5. Automatic Lesson Thumbnails from Chapters
* **The Problem:** Having to design and upload 30 individual thumbnail pictures for every single lesson takes too much time.
* **The Solution:** 
  1. If you don't upload a thumbnail for a lesson, the system will automatically use the Chapter's stylized cover image.
  2. You can also click a single button: *"Apply Chapter Cover to all Lessons in this Chapter"* to set them all at once.
* **The Benefit for You:** Your courses will always look complete, professional, and visually consistent without uploading duplicate images.
