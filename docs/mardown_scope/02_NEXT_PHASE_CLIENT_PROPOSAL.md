## 1. International Payment with PayPal QR

### The Opportunity

Currently, ArchTipsBox only accepts Cambodian Bakong KHQR and manual Telegram contacts. Non-Cambodian students who want to purchase courses or subscriptions have no easy checkout option.

### How It Works for Your Students

1. At checkout, international students choose the **"PayPal (International)"** option.
2. They scan your verified **PayPal QR Code** with their phone camera or click your instant `paypal.me` link.
3. They submit their PayPal transaction ID or upload a screenshot of their receipt.
4. **Instant Admin Verification:** In your Admin Panel, you get an alert showing new pending international payments. You click **"Approve & Enroll"** with one tap, and the student receives their welcome email and immediate access.

### Business Value

- Expand your student base across Southeast Asia, Europe, and the US.
- Zero complex international merchant setup; uses your existing PayPal account directly.

## 2. Device Limit & Anti-Account Sharing (Maximum 2 Devices)

### The Threat

In online learning platforms, course piracy usually doesn't start on torrent sites—it starts with **account sharing**. One person buys a subscription and gives their login to 3 to 5 colleagues or classmates.

### The Solution: 2-Device Smart Lock

- Each student account is restricted to a maximum of **2 active devices** at any time (for example: their Workstation Desktop + their Laptop or iPad).
- **What happens if someone shares their account?**
  - If a 3rd person tries to watch a video, the system automatically stops them or logs out the oldest device with a polite warning:
    > _"Device limit reached (2 devices active). You have been logged out on your other device."_
- **Student Self-Service:**
  Students can easily see and manage their active devices inside their Account Settings page.

### Business Value

- Protects your intellectual property and 3D tutorial assets.
- Directly increases course sales by converting freeloaders into paying students.

## 3. Visual Drag-and-Drop Course Studio

### The Problem

When you upload a new lesson today and want to insert it between Lesson 2 and Lesson 3, you have to manually edit every single lesson number one by one.

### The Solution

- You can now grab any lesson with your mouse and drag it anywhere you want—up, down, or even into another chapter.
- Once you release the mouse, the entire course automatically re-numbers itself in the background (1, 2, 3, 4...).

### Business Value

- Saves hours of manual work when updating course materials.
- Makes restructuring courses feel modern, visual, and fun.
